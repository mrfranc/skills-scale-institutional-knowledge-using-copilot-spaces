# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Owner / Business Sponsor

### Role Summary
Product Owners and Business Sponsors define business outcomes, funding priorities, and strategic trade-offs so the team can make the right decisions about scope, timing, and value.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify business goals, success metrics, and desired outcomes
- Prioritize work based on value, urgency, and stakeholder impact
- Confirm scope and trade-offs when constraints arise
- Partner with project and product leads on roadmap alignment
- Support stakeholder decisions and escalation where business priorities conflict

### Goals
- Maximize business value delivery
- Keep project outputs aligned with strategic goals
- Ensure the team is solving the right problem

### Typical Communication
- Roadmap reviews and business prioritization meetings
- Decisions on scope, trade-offs, and funding constraints
- Regular updates to executive sponsors and business stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to refine priorities and expected user value.
- Partners with Project Managers to confirm trade-offs, timelines, and cross-team dependencies.
- Aligns with Developers and Delivery Managers to ensure the work remains feasible and valuable.

---

## Delivery Manager / Program Lead

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers and Program Leads coordinate execution across workstreams, dependencies, and teams so work can move predictably and blockers are addressed early.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate delivery across multiple teams or workstreams
- Track dependencies, milestone risks, and delivery health
- Help resolve bottlenecks, resource conflicts, and execution issues
- Maintain visibility into progress against plans and commitments
- Support escalation paths for cross-functional issues

### Goals
- Improve delivery predictability and execution alignment
- Reduce friction across teams and dependencies
- Keep work moving toward agreed milestones

### Typical Communication
- Cross-team planning and dependency reviews
- Status checkpoints with project leads and stakeholders
- Escalation updates when risks affect sprint or release commitments

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to maintain plans and dependency tracking.
- Collaborates with Developers to confirm delivery feasibility and identify risks early.
- Provides Product Managers with execution context on trade-offs, sequencing, and delivery readiness.

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness, stakeholder communication, and deployment sequencing to reduce risk and ensure a controlled, well-understood delivery process.

### Responsibilities
- Define release readiness criteria and checkpoints
- Coordinate deployment plans with engineering, operations, and stakeholders
- Validate communication, rollback readiness, and support preparation
- Manage release timing and risk communication
- Track post-release issues and coordinate follow-up actions

### Goals
- Deliver releases with minimal disruption
- Improve release predictability and stakeholder confidence
- Reduce operational and communication risk during deployment

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment checklists
- Stakeholder communications before, during, and after release
- Post-release review and issue follow-up conversations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Project Managers on schedule alignment and communication plans.
- Works with Developers and Delivery Managers to confirm deployment readiness and known issues.
- Coordinates with Product Owners and stakeholders on business-facing release announcements and risk messaging.

---

## Risk and Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Risk and Compliance Leads identify project risks, control exposures, and mitigation strategies so teams can make informed decisions and manage critical issues before they escalate.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and assess project, operational, and compliance risks
- Maintain risk registers and escalation criteria
- Partner with leaders to define mitigation actions and ownership
- Monitor policy, security, and regulatory concerns relevant to the project
- Support issue triage when a risk affects delivery or governance

### Goals
- Reduce avoidable project disruption and policy exposure
- Increase visibility into emerging issues and dependencies
- Improve decision quality through informed risk management

### Typical Communication
- Risk review meetings and governance check-ins
- Issue escalation summaries and mitigation status updates
- Security, compliance, and operational advisories

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to track risk status and mitigation ownership.
- Partners with Product Owners and Delivery Managers on trade-offs between speed, scope, and risk.
- Supports Developers and Release Managers by clarifying compliance requirements and risk controls.

---

## Stakeholder / Communication Lead

### Role Summary
Stakeholder and Communication Leads ensure the right people receive timely, consistent, and accurate information about project status, decisions, and impact.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain stakeholder communication plans
- Tailor updates to the needs of executives, teams, and partners
- Coordinate status reporting and decision summaries
- Surface issues requiring attention or alignment from leadership
- Help create trust and transparency around progress and risks

### Goals
- Keep stakeholders informed and aligned
- Reduce surprises and misunderstandings
- Improve collaboration across project participants

### Typical Communication
- Executive summaries and project updates
- Decision logs and stakeholder briefing notes
- Cross-functional communication and escalation notices

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to ensure status updates are accurate and timely.
- Partners with Product Owners and Release Managers to communicate changes in priorities, commitments, or launch plans.
- Supports Developers and Delivery Managers by translating technical progress into clear business context.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These additional roles help clarify accountability, improve handoffs, and reinforce decision-making in cross-functional work.
